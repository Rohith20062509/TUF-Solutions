## 📝 Problem Statement

Given an integer array nums, find three numbers whose product is maximum and return the maximum product.

### Example 1:

**Input:** nums = [1, 2, 3]

**Output:** 6

**Explanation:** 1 × 2 × 3 = 6

### Example 2:

**Input:** nums = [1, 2, 3, 4]

**Output:** 24

**Explanation:** 2 × 3 × 4 = 24
